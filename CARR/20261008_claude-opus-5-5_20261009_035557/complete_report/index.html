Summary¶

CARR is in a long-term downtrend that has paused. It is below both its 50-day and 200-day averages, and the trend-following line (SuperTrend) points down on the daily, weekly and monthly charts. The selling has run out of strength, though. Trend strength has fallen from 36.8 to 15.3, short-term momentum turned up on Sep 23, and the weekly exhaustion count is complete. Since mid-September the price has been moving sideways in a band of about 54.0 to 56.8, with a flat top and slightly rising lows. Volume has not confirmed a turn yet.

Technical lean: neutral to bearish; wait for a break out of the range. At 55.83 the price sits in the middle of that range. A trade from here has poor reward for its risk in either direction. The better setups are at the edges of the range or after a confirmed break.
