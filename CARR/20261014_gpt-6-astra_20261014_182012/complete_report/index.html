4. Risk controls and scope¶

For any candidate CARR setup, size should follow the actual entry-to-stop distance:

Position size = chosen dollar risk ÷ estimated per-share loss at the stop, allowing for transaction costs and slippage.

ATR is not a maximum-loss guarantee, and stop orders can fill beyond their trigger during gaps. Weekly and monthly SuperTrend references are particularly distant and should not be imported into a short-horizon trade without materially adjusting size.

This is a technical assessment. The tools used here do not verify earnings dates, current news, valuation, options positioning, or upcoming catalysts. Those checks remain necessary before an execution decision.
