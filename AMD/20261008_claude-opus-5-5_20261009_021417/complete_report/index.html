AMD (Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.): Technical Report¶

1. Bottom line¶

AMD's main trend is still strongly up. The short-term picture has weakened, though. The breakout to a new high on 2026-10-06 is failing. On 10/08 the stock fell −3.90% (645.86 → 620.68) and closed near the day's low. That close is back inside the late-September trading range and just under the 10-day EMA. RSI, MACD and ADX all failed to confirm the 10/06 high, and the buying pressure measured by OBV during the breakout has been fully given back. The weekly and monthly charts are stretched but not extreme yet. The most likely next step is a pullback or sideways period within the uptrend. Nothing yet signals that the trend itself is reversing.
