4. Risk considerations and decision handoff¶

For AMD, the volatility evidence argues against treating narrow price differences as robust protection:

  • From the latest close, the October 22 closing reference of 507.93 is only 8.86 away, much less than one current ATR.
  • A stop near a nearby reference may therefore sit inside normal recent volatility.
  • Conversely, using a distant weekly stop for a short-term position can create substantially more risk than intended.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = chosen dollar-risk budget ÷ entry-to-stop distance

Actual losses can exceed that calculation because of gaps, slippage, and execution conditions. Stop placement should reflect the specific setup; ATR should help assess its scale rather than mechanically replace it.

Overall AMD handoff: The tools support a cautious tactical outlook with a short-term exhaustion watch. They do not yet establish a bullish reversal, but the intact weekly trend and completed daily TD-9 also argue against assuming uninterrupted downside.
