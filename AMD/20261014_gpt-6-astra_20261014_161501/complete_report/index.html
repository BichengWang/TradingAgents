7. Risk management and limitations¶

The distance to the weekly stop is large: a decline from 570.82 to 453.83 would equal approximately 20.50% of the latest close. The monthly stop is approximately 35.79% below the latest close.

These percentages use the current close as the denominator; the SuperTrend tool’s reported distances use the stop line as the denominator.

This makes position sizing essential. A structurally valid weekly stop can still imply excessive risk for a short-term position.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted dollar loss ÷ entry-to-stop distance per share

The distance should account for execution costs and potential slippage. An ATR multiple can inform that calculation, but it should not replace a coherent invalidation level.

This analysis does not evaluate AMD’s valuation, earnings outlook, upcoming catalysts, options positioning, or market-wide semiconductor conditions. No historical win rate or backtested strategy performance has been established.

The strongest next evidence would be either a sustained recovery with improving momentum and participation, or a confirmed downside extension that begins to weaken the medium-term trend.
