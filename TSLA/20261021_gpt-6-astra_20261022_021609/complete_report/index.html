5. Volatility and risk management¶

Verified ATR is 12.42 per share. This measures recent true-range volatility; it is not a guaranteed daily movement or a probability boundary.

For scale:

  • 1.5 ATR ≈ 18.63
  • 2 ATR ≈ 24.84

A tight stop placed only a few dollars from an entry could therefore sit well inside ordinary recent price variation.

At the latest close, the daily SuperTrend line at 348.57 is 37.90 below price, or approximately three ATRs. Using that structural line as a stop reference would require materially smaller position sizing than using a narrower, independently justified invalidation level.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = maximum acceptable dollar loss ÷ absolute distance between entry and stop

Allow for slippage and gaps: an actual execution loss can exceed the planned stop distance.

Importantly, low ADX does not mean low risk. TSLA can experience substantial volatility while lacking a persistent directional trend.
