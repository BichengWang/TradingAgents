For illustration only—not as an entry recommendation:

Position sizing should use the actual intended entry and exit:

Position size = permitted dollar risk ÷ per-share risk, rounded down and adjusted for execution uncertainty.

A close-based trend exit and an intraday hard stop are different strategies. The former accepts intraday excursions and potential overnight gaps; the latter may exit during ordinary volatility.
