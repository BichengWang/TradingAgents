10. Scenarios and what each tells you¶

Overall lean: neutral to constructive. The longer-term picture is bullish: SuperTrend is up on all three timeframes, the golden cross is new, and the 50-day SMA is rising. The short-term picture is a correction: MACD is below its signal, price is below the 10-day EMA, highs are getting lower, and OBV is fading. When timeframes disagree, the higher one gets more weight, so this reads as a pullback inside an uptrend, not a reversal, as long as $680.67 holds.

  1. Bullish resolution (needs confirmation). The $711–$716 floor holds, price closes back above the 10-day EMA (726.12), then breaks above $747.60 on above-average volume (ideally over 20M). That would complete the pause and aim for the $749–$757 old-high zone, then $777.59–$786.65. The MACD histogram turning back up and RSI back above about 64 would confirm. Rough math: buying a close above $747.60 with a stop near $724 risks about $23 (1× ATR) for $30–$39 of upside, roughly 1.3–1.7 to 1.

  2. Continued sideways (most likely in the near term). Price chops between $711 and $748 while ATR and the Bollinger Bands narrow into the probable earnings window. MACD drifts lower toward zero. Range trading can work but needs tight stops given the event risk.

  3. Deeper pullback (triggered by a close below $711.68). A daily close below the triangle floor and the Bollinger middle line would likely bring a quick move to $688 (38.2%), then the $679.60–$680.67 stop zone. Rough math for a dip buy: buying around $688 with a stop below $669 (about 0.5 ATR under the SuperTrend line) risks about $19 for $59–$89 back to $747–$777, better than 3 to 1. This is the best risk/reward in the current setup.

  4. Trend damage (triggered by a close below $680.67). The daily SuperTrend would flip down and the 09-21 breakout day would be fully undone. Targets would be $660–$665, then $644, then the $624–$638 gap and SMA cluster. Even then the weekly and monthly SuperTrend would still read up (stops at 565.33 and 522.45), so this would be a deep correction within the larger uptrend unless the $624 area also gives way.

Practical notes: - Existing longs: the trend is intact. Keep the stop below $679 (just under the daily SuperTrend line). For a tighter version, cut part of the position on a close below $711. - New longs: don't chase at $720–$726 while price is below the 10-day EMA and MACD is falling. Good entry areas are $711–$716 (aggressive) or $680–$688 (better risk/reward), or a confirmed close above $747.60. - Shorts: nothing supports a short above $680.67. The weekly z-score of +1.66 is not extreme enough to bet against an uptrend confirmed on all three timeframes. - Size: keep positions smaller ahead of late October, since the past four quarterly gaps averaged 3× the current ATR.
