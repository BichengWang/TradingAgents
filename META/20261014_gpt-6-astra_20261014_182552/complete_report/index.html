For any candidate setup, position sizing should follow the actual entry and invalidation level:

Position size ≈ allowable dollar loss ÷ (entry price − stop price)

Allow separately for slippage and gaps. ATR measures recent movement; it is not a maximum-loss boundary, and a stop order does not guarantee execution at its trigger price.
