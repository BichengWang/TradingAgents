Useful planning principles:

Position size ≤ chosen dollar-risk budget ÷ absolute distance between planned entry and stop

2. “MaxLinear is entering AECs” is a serious challenge—not proof that CRDO’s advantage has disappeared¶

This is the strongest competitive objection I expect, and I would not dismiss it.

A growing market can attract competitors, reduce an incumbent’s share, and pressure pricing. Strong AI spending alone does not protect CRDO’s economics.

But the supplied evidence establishes competitive entry and investor concern, not verified customer displacement. The distinction is consequential:

Product announcement → customer qualification → production deployment → revenue capture → margin impact.

Those steps cannot simply be collapsed into “CRDO has lost its moat.”

In high-speed connectivity, customers generally evaluate reliability, signal integrity, power consumption, and system integration—not just component price. Qualification experience and execution can favor an incumbent. That is an industry-level reason an established supplier may retain advantages, not proof of specific CRDO contract protections.

What company-specific evidence supports the bull interpretation?

  • Headlines describe CRDO as the incumbent in the AEC market, although they do not independently establish market share.
  • CRDO has demonstrated the ability to scale revenue rapidly.
  • Its latest 64.5% gross margin indicates substantial gross profitability, although margins alone cannot establish a durable technological moat.

The bullish claim is therefore not “competition does not matter.” It is: CRDO has demonstrated commercial execution and attractive economics, while the extent of competitive damage remains unverified.

The reported $4 billion Super Micro forecast increase provides a supportive end-market signal. It is not a CRDO order, but it argues against assuming that competitive concerns are arriving alongside a demonstrated collapse in AI-platform demand.

  • Operating margin declined from approximately 35.5% to 25.3%.
