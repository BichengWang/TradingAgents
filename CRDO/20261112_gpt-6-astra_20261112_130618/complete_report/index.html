A particularly important reward/risk observation is that the distance from 140.66 to the upper edge of the first recovery hurdle at 151.15 is only 10.49, less than one ATR. A trade targeting only that hurdle could therefore offer limited reward relative to ordinary price movement.

The appropriate sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted monetary loss ÷ distance from actual entry to the chosen invalidation level

That calculation should allow for fees, slippage, and gap risk. ATR does not replace structural invalidation, and a stop order does not guarantee its execution price.

This analysis does not assess earnings catalysts, valuation, options positioning, or short-borrow availability.
