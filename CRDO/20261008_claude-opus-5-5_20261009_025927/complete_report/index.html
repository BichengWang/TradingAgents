1. Summary¶

CRDO is in the middle of a strong V-shaped recovery. On 2026-09-02 the stock fell 20.0% in one session, from 206.63 to 165.22, on 30.1M shares. It bottomed at a 150.09 close on 2026-09-14 and has since risen 41.2% to 211.86. All three SuperTrend timeframes (weekly, monthly, daily) point up. Price is above the 10 EMA, the 50 SMA and the 200 SMA. +DI has led –DI since 2026-09-22, and ADX has climbed from 7.82 to 24.68, just under the usual 25 threshold for a confirmed trend.

Over the last four sessions, though, short-term momentum has cooled. MFI fell from 81.6 to 60.2, +DI dropped from 41.65 to 33.28, OBV gave back about 17M shares, and the daily highs have stepped down from the 231.59 swing high. Today's bar rejected the 224 area on the heaviest volume since 2026-10-02.

Bottom line: the intermediate-term picture is constructive and the short term is a consolidation. The 206–226 range is the decision zone. Neither the TD count nor the z-score shows any exhaustion, so the uptrend has room if buyers defend 206.
