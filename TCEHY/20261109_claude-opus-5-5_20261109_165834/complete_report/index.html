2. The bear's best argument: Q2 earnings quality¶

I'll concede the facts first. Q2 opex grew 25.3% against revenue growth of 11%. Operating margin fell 1.9 points. Reported net income was up only 0.7%, helped by larger unusual gains, and normalized net income fell about 13%.
