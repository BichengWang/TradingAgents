1. Why these indicators¶

The snapshot already covers the moving averages, Bollinger bands, RSI, MACD and ATR. I added indicators that cover what it lacks: - SuperTrend (weekly/monthly/daily): sets the regime hierarchy and gives concrete flip levels. - ADX: tells me whether the current move is a real trend. - RSI and MACD: two different momentum views, a bounded oscillator and a trend-momentum crossover. - ATR: volatility for stop and position sizing. - OBV: checks whether volume supports the rally. - TD-9: shows how far along any exhaustion count is. - Z-Score: shows how stretched price is on three timeframes.

I left out stochastic variants because they overlap with RSI.
