Executive assessment¶

  • Longer-term reversal: Not confirmed.

6. ATR: risk distances remain meaningful¶

atr: 12.87¶

The verified daily ATR is 12.87. This is a volatility measure, not a directional forecast or a guaranteed daily trading range.

Its practical importance is that small price fluctuations can be material in dollar terms without representing a genuine trend change. Stops placed only slightly beyond an entry or a nearby pivot can be vulnerable to ordinary volatility.

A useful hypothetical distance comparison illustrates the current challenge:

  • Latest close: 343.39
  • Daily SuperTrend line: 310.84
  • Distance down to that line: 32.55
  • Distance up to the current 200-day average, 364.61: 21.22

Therefore, a hypothetical fresh long using the daily SuperTrend line as its sole risk reference has more distance to that reference than to the first major moving-average checkpoint. This does not rule out a trade, but it argues against assuming that the current close offers attractive reward-to-risk under that particular framework.

These are reference-distance calculations, not executable fill assumptions. Actual stop fills can differ because of gaps and slippage.

For position sizing:

Shares = planned dollar risk ÷ distance between actual entry and planned stop

The stop should reflect the trade’s invalidation condition and volatility—not simply the desired position size.
