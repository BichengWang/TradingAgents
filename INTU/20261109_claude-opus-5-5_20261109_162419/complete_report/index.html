1. Bottom line¶

The higher-timeframe trend for INTU is still down. Since the mid-October low, a counter-trend rebound has run into a cluster of overhead resistance, and momentum is now fading. Daily structure is still constructive, but weekly and monthly structure are bearish.

  • Price is in the middle of its near-term range, not stretched in either direction.
  • It sits between the 50 SMA (317.44) below and the 200 SMA (351.92) above.
  • Three sessions of give-back from the 2026-11-04 swing high have pulled MACD, RSI and OBV off their recent peaks.
  • The next decision zones are about 317 on the downside and about 352–366 on the upside.
