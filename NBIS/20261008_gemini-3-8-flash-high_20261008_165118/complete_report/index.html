To deliver comprehensive market insight without analytical redundancy, eight complementary technical indicators were chosen across five distinct categories:

  4. MACD Histogram (macdh) [Momentum / Crossover Dynamics]: Measures the rate of change between the MACD line and signal line, flagging early shifts in momentum before moving averages lag.
  5. Relative Strength Index (rsi) [Momentum Oscillator]: Gauges standard 14-period directional momentum on a 0–100 scale to identify overbought/oversold exhaustion without stochastic noise.
  6. Bollinger Bands (boll, boll_ub, boll_lb) [Volatility Envelope]: Defines dynamic two-standard-deviation envelopes around the 20 SMA to capture statistical volatility expansion and potential mean-reversion zones.
  7. Volume-Weighted Moving Average (vwma) [Volume-Price Integration]: Integrates institutional volume weighting to establish the real volume-weighted cost basis of recent market participants.
  8. Z-Score (z_score) [Mean Reversion / Statistical Stretch]: Measures how many standard deviations the close sits from its 20-period mean across daily, weekly, and monthly timeframes to detect extreme extensions (|z| ≥ 2.0).
