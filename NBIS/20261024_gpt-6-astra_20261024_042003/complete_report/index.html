Executive assessment¶

The practical distinction is important: evidence that selling may be tiring is not the same as evidence that buyers have regained control.

I selected eight complementary indicators:

  8. z_score — measures stretch relative to the rolling price distribution.

This avoids stacking several similar oscillators or multiple versions of the same momentum signal.

7. Conditional framework for NBIS¶

A. Bearish continuation¶

The dominant trend would receive additional confirmation from either:

  • A sustained break below 155.73, followed by a failed reclaim; or
  • A rebound toward 170.24–175.88 that produces a clear rejection and lower high.

The caution is entry quality. Entering immediately above the recent lows, after a completed daily TD-9 setup, exposes a new bearish position to countertrend rebound risk.

If lower historical references offer insufficient potential reward relative to a volatility-appropriate stop, passing on the setup is preferable to forcing a tighter stop.

B. Countertrend rebound¶

A more credible rebound setup would involve several conditions together:

  1. NBIS reclaims 162.99.
  2. A subsequent pullback forms a higher low rather than immediately losing the recent low zone.
  3. RSI turns upward.
  4. OBV begins improving alongside price.

The 170.24–171.57 area and then the evolving 200-day SMA would be recovery checkpoints.

Even if those conditions appear, the move would initially remain countertrend while weekly and daily SuperTrend remain down.

C. More durable repair¶

A stronger recovery case would require:

  • Sustained trading above the evolving 200-day SMA.
  • A higher-low price structure.
  • Improving momentum and volume participation.
  • Eventual improvement in daily, and then primary weekly, trend signals.

A single strong session would not establish all of these conditions.

Bottom line¶

NBIS currently favors a bearish trend interpretation, qualified by meaningful short-term exhaustion warnings. The evidence supports monitoring conditional setups rather than assuming either an immediate bottom or uninterrupted downside continuation.
