Executive assessment¶

ORCL remains in a bearish multi-timeframe structure, but its recent rebound and developing weekly exhaustion setup argue against treating the downside as an uncomplicated trend-following opportunity.

ORCL has recovered from its early-November closing lows, but that recovery has not restored the medium-term trend. Price remains below its declining 50-day average, and weekly, monthly, and daily SuperTrend readings all remain down.

Decision-useful interpretation: ORCL currently presents an unconfirmed countertrend recovery inside a bearish broader structure. Both chasing the rebound and initiating fresh shorts without confirmation carry meaningful technical risks.

Risk controls and limitations¶

A practical sizing framework is:

Position size = maximum acceptable dollar loss ÷ planned per-share risk

Planned risk should reflect the entry-to-invalidation distance, with allowances for slippage and gap risk. Stop orders cannot guarantee the intended loss limit.
