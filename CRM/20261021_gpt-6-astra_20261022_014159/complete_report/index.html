Upside-confirmation scenario¶

For CRM breakout consideration, look for a combination of:

  1. A close through 246.74, followed by a close above the then-current daily SuperTrend line.
  2. RSI remaining above 50.
  3. ADX strengthening toward or above 25.
  4. OBV improving beyond its October 15 local high.

That combination would align price, momentum, trend strength, and participation more convincingly. A one-session price spike without participation would be weaker evidence.

Pullback-and-stabilization scenario¶

If CRM retreats toward the 232.70–233.92 reference area, the important question is whether it stabilizes and reclaims nearby levels—not merely whether it touches the moving average.

A more constructive pullback would feature:

  • A closing recovery rather than continued deterioration.
  • RSI holding above, or recovering through, 50.
  • OBV stabilizing rather than making progressively lower readings.

A touch of the 50-day SMA alone would not establish a reversal.

Recovery-failure scenario¶

CRM’s near-term recovery would weaken more materially if it:

  • Loses 237.23 and subsequently remains below the 50-day SMA;
  • Shows RSI persisting below 50; and
  • Records further OBV deterioration.

That would shift attention toward the deeper closing-price references. Even then, weakness on the daily timeframe would not automatically mean the weekly SuperTrend has reversed.
