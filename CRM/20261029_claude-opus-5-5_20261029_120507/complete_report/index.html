At about $232 the forward P/E is 14.5x and the PEG is 0.86. For a company with this cash profile and this much scale, that is not a priced-for-perfection multiple.

The balance sheet shows equity falling about $25B in Q1 FY27 while liabilities jumped. That looks like a debt-funded repurchase, though the tools don't give buyback line items, so it's my inference.
