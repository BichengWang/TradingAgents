The indicator history shows ATR at 27.28 on 2026-08-10, compared with 19.77 now. Volatility has moderated relative to that point, but BE is not a low-volatility instrument.

For BE risk planning:

  • A stop much tighter than one ATR can be exposed to ordinary price noise.
  • A wider stop requires a smaller position if the permitted dollar loss is unchanged.
  • SuperTrend stops should be matched to the intended holding period.
  • Overnight gaps and slippage can cause realized losses to exceed the planned amount.

A basic sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted dollar risk ÷ distance between actual entry and planned stop

ATR is not a forecast or a maximum daily movement. Its role here is to prevent an apparently modest price-level stop from disguising significant risk.
