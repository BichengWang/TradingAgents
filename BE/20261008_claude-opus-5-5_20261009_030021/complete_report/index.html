Look for stabilization before buying, such as the MACD histogram turning back up or RSI holding about 40–45. Risk to $233.93 from $255 is about $21, roughly one ATR, which is a much better reward-to-risk than buying at today's mid-range price.

  5. Position sizing. With ATR at about $19.8, risking $10,000 against the $233.93 stop (about $38.9 per share) works out to about 257 shares. Set size from that dollar risk rather than a fixed share count.
  6. Event risk. This data shows quarterly-spaced single-day jumps: +24.0% on 4/14, +27.2% on 4/29 and +26.5% on 7/30, plus the −18.5% drop on 6/26.
