"The multiple is absurd: 117x PE, 29.6x book, 15x sales." It is expensive, and I'm not claiming otherwise. But the multiple has already compressed 44% from the peak, and earnings are compounding faster than the price is falling. A stock that grows revenue 140% with 18% operating margins and ROE near 40% deserves a premium.

"Sequential growth slowed from +42% to +16%." That is real. But +16% sequential growth on a $1B base compounds to roughly 80% annualized. Revenue is also lumpy: Q1'26 dipped below Q4'25, then Q2 surged. One quarter is not a trend.
