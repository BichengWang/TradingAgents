7. Risk controls and limitations¶

  • Define invalidation before entry. A nearby price reference is not automatically a sensible stop when ATR is approximately 2.87.
  • Size from the stop distance: Position size = predefined dollar risk ÷ absolute entry-to-stop distance.
  • Check reward relative to risk. A correct directional view can still produce an unattractive setup when the next objective is too close.
  • Allow for gaps and slippage. Stop orders do not guarantee the intended execution price.
  • Confirm higher-timeframe signals at the relevant close. The outputs do not specify whether incomplete weekly or monthly bars are included.
  • Keep the scope technical. These tools do not establish earnings timing, current news catalysts, valuation, options positioning, or short-borrow conditions.

Overall assessment for BABA: The broader bias remains bearish, but weak daily ADX argues against indiscriminate momentum entries. A more favorable technical opportunity would come from either a confirmed failed rebound, a well-supported reclaim, or a breakdown with stronger participation—not from assuming that the current price alone is a sufficient signal.
