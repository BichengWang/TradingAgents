MRVL (Marvell Technology, Inc.): Technical Analysis Report¶

1. Summary¶

MRVL has recovered strongly from its July collapse, but it is now sitting just below the line that matters most on the weekly chart.

  • Daily and monthly trends point up. Price is above the 10 EMA, 20 SMA, 50 SMA and 200 SMA. MACD is positive, and the daily lows keep getting higher.
  • The weekly trend, which gets the most weight, has not turned up yet. The weekly SuperTrend still reads DOWN, with its line at 286.55. A weekly close above that level would flip it.
  • Several signs of a tiring move showed up this week:
  • The weekly TD-9 has completed a sell setup (count -9), and the daily count is at -8.
  • The Oct 6 candle rose to an intraday high of 301.27 but closed at 287.01.
  • Two down days on above-average volume followed, erasing all of OBV's (on-balance volume) Oct 6 gain.

Overall: the trend is healthy but stretched, and it is testing resistance. Buying here gives a poor reward relative to risk. The better setups are either a pullback into the rising 256–262 support zone or a weekly close above 286.55.
