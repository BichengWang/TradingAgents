1. The big picture¶

XBI is in a short-term downtrend inside an intermediate- and long-term uptrend that is still intact.

  • The rally: XBI rose from a 118.66 close on 2026-03-30 to a 169.55 close on 2026-08-19, a gain of about +42.9%.
  • The top: It then stalled in the 162–169 zone through late August. The highest intraday print was 169.89 on 2026-08-25.
  • The range: Through September it traded in a narrowing range, with closes roughly between 154 and 162. The low end was 154.03 on 09-15 and the high end 161.87 on 09-22.
  • The breakdown (2026-10-06): XBI fell from 156.19 to 150.90 (−3.39%) on 17.22M shares. That is the heaviest volume since 2026-04-01 (20.03M) and it pushed price below the September range floor.
  • Follow-through: Two more lower closes came next, 150.23 and 149.29. Today's close is the lowest since 2026-08-03 (147.31).
  • Drawdown: XBI is about −11.9% below the 169.55 closing high.

The daily z-score of −2.11 and the close below the lower band suggest a bounce toward the mean is likely soon. The daily TD count is close to a 9, and buyers defended the 146 zone intraday.
  • Net: The short term favors sellers, but price is stretched on the daily. The odds favor a pause or bounce before long, inside a correction that has not finished, not an immediate V-shaped recovery. Since momentum and volume have not turned, it looks too early to buy in size on the stretch readings alone.

  2. a daily TD 9 completing near 146–150

  5. a close back above 153.44–154, the 10 EMA and the old range floor

Regime change: A weekly close below ~139.3 would flip the weekly SuperTrend and break the 200 SMA. The higher-timeframe view would then turn from "pullback in an uptrend" to "trend reversal."
