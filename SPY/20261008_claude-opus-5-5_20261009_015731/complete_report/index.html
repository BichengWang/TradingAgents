SPY (State Street SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust, PCX): Technical Report¶

Summary¶

SPY is in a strong long-term uptrend. It rose from 651.94 on Apr 1 to 773.93 now, a gain of about 18.7%. For the last two months it has moved sideways. On Oct 6 it broke above that range with a close of 779.09, but it has lost ground on the two days since and is back just under the top of the range. Momentum and volume both look weaker than in August, and the trend-strength reading (ADX) is very low. The trend still favours buyers, but the evidence argues for waiting for confirmation rather than buying the breakout now. Which way to trade is for the decision-making agent to choose.

  • Uptrend leg (Apr to early Aug): price rose from 651.94 (Apr 1) to 769.42 (Aug 4). A sharp drop in June (low of 714.81 on Jun 26) did not break the uptrend.
  • Sideways range (Aug 4 to Oct 1): closes stayed between 752.18 (Sep 16) and 775.95 (Aug 13). The lowest intraday price was 747.74 (Sep 16) and the highest was 777.44 (Aug 13). From Aug 4 (769.42) to today, price is up only about 0.6%.
  • Breakout attempt: on Oct 6 SPY closed at a new high of 779.09, with an intraday high of 781.62. It then closed at 777.22 on Oct 7, just under the Aug 13 high of 777.44, and at 773.93 on Oct 8, back below the Aug 13 closing high of 775.95. That is a 5.16-point (‑0.66%) drop from the Oct 6 close.
  • What this means: the breakout is still unconfirmed. SPY is now just under the old ceiling at 775–777.5 (Aug 13 high 777.44; Sep 22 high 775.14). If it gets back above 777.44–779.09, the breakout is back on. If it keeps falling, this starts to look like a failed breakout.

  • 200 SMA = 719.16, also rising (698.93 on Aug 10). Price is 54.77 points (7.6%) above it.
  • The 50 SMA sits 46.35 points above the 200 SMA, and both slopes are positive. The 50-over-200 bullish alignment holds, and nothing points to the two lines crossing soon.
  • 10 EMA = 771.56: price is only 2.37 points above it. A close below it would be an early sign that short-term momentum is fading.
  • Takeaway: the primary trend is clearly bullish. On a pullback, the 765–767 zone (50 SMA plus the Bollinger midline at 766.79) is the first important medium-term support.
