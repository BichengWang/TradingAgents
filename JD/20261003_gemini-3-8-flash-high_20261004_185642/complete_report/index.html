1. Executive Summary¶

JD.com, Inc. (JD) is one of China’s largest supply chain-based technology and e-commerce enterprises, renowned for its proprietary nationwide logistics network, direct sales (1P) retail model, and expanding third-party (3P) marketplace ecosystem.

Over the historical horizon culminating in FY2025, JD demonstrated sustained top-line expansion, surpassing RMB 1.309 trillion in annual revenue in FY2025—an acceleration to +12.97% year-over-year (YoY) growth, compared to +6.84% in FY2024 and +3.67% in FY2023. Gross margins have exhibited multi-year structural expansion, climbing from 13.56% in FY2021 to 16.04% in FY2025, underscoring supply chain optimization and scale efficiencies.

However, operating profitability experienced sharp volatility in FY2025. Following record operating income of RMB 38.74 billion and diluted EPS of RMB 13.43 in FY2024, FY2025 operating income compressed to RMB 2.77 billion (diluted EPS of RMB 6.45). This contraction highlights heightened competition across Chinese domestic e-commerce, marked by substantial price-subsidies, merchant incentives, and reinvestments into user acquisition and ecosystem services.

Despite operational margin pressure, JD maintains a fortress balance sheet, closing FY2025 with RMB 137.49 billion in cash and cash equivalents (up from RMB 108.35 billion in FY2024), a current ratio of 1.22x, and RMB 225.04 billion in stockholders' equity. Financing cash outflows of RMB 26.73 billion in FY2025 and RMB 21.00 billion in FY2024 affirm management's commitment to substantial capital returns via share repurchases and cash dividends.

2. Corporate Profile & Business Model¶

JD operates an integrated retail and supply chain model that distinguishes it from asset-light marketplace peers:

  1. JD Retail (JD Mall & 1P Direct Sales): The core engine of the company, focusing on consumer electronics, home appliances, general merchandise, FMCG (fast-moving consumer goods), fresh food, and luxury goods. JD purchases inventory directly from manufacturers, guaranteeing authenticity, standardized customer service, and rapid fulfillment.
  2. Third-Party Marketplace (3P Platform): Complementing the 1P model, JD hosts third-party merchants, earning commission, advertising, and fulfillment service fees. The expansion of 3P merchants has supported overall gross margin enhancement.
  3. JD Logistics (JDL): A proprietary, vertically integrated logistics and supply chain network consisting of hundreds of mega-warehouses, automated "Asia No. 1" fulfillment centers, cold-chain networks, and last-mile delivery couriers. JDL serves both JD Retail and external enterprise clients.
  4. New Businesses & Technology Initiatives: Includes JD Health (online healthcare, digital pharmacy), JD Technology (fintech, supply chain finance, enterprise cloud), and cross-border/omnichannel retail initiatives.
