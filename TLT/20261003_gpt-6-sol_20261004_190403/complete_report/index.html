TLT: bearish trend, but increasingly stretched¶

Even then, frame the initial move as a countertrend trade while TLT remains below the 50-day SMA at USD 81.15 and weekly SuperTrend at USD 82.64. Require stronger evidence before treating it as a sustained trend reversal.

For a prospective short: The aligned trend supports a bearish bias, but oversold RSI and stretched weekly/monthly z-scores make an immediate entry less attractive. Size any position against a predefined stop and the current ATR.
