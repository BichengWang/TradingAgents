II. Research Team Decision¶

Bull Researcher¶

Bull Analyst: # Bull Case for LLY (Eli Lilly): Opening Argument

I'll start with the numbers, then take on the objections I expect from the bear.

1. This is a rare combination of scale and acceleration¶

  • Revenue: Q2 2026 revenue was $22.97B, up 47.7% year over year and 16% sequentially. Lilly is growing at nearly 50% with a $79.7B trailing revenue base. Quarterly revenue is 3.5x its Q2 2022 level.
  • Margins are expanding: Gross margin went from 76.8% in 2022 to 83.0% in 2025 and hit a series-high 85.8% in Q2. Cost of revenue grew 33.5% while sales grew 47.7%. That is operating leverage at scale.
  • Earnings: H1 2026 net income was $14.5B, up 72% on revenue up 51%. EPS has compounded at about 49% a year since 2022.
  • Cash: Q2 operating cash flow was $10.7B, about 150% of net income. TTM operating cash flow is roughly $28B, about 105% of TTM earnings. These are cash profits, not accounting ones.

2. Valuation is demanding, but it's priced for the growth¶

The bear will point to the 42.7x trailing P/E. That figure uses a trailing base that Lilly has already outgrown:

  • Q2 annualized revenue is about $92B against $79.7B TTM. On run-rate sales the stock trades at about 12.4x, not 14.2x.
  • Forward P/E is 27x and PEG is 1.26. I'll concede that the $47.22 forward EPS is probably a non-GAAP consensus figure and shouldn't be compared directly with GAAP TTM EPS. Even so, a PEG near 1.3 for a company growing revenue about 48% is not a bubble multiple.
  • Beta is 0.45. Investors are paying a premium for a durable, low-beta compounder.

3. The technical picture is a trend, not a spike¶

  • Structure: The moving averages stack in the right order (price 1273 > 10 EMA 1250 > 50 SMA 1187 > 200 SMA 1083). SuperTrend is up on the weekly, monthly and daily tiers. ADX is about 45, up from 19 on Oct 13.
  • Volume: The breakout from the September base (1116–1189) to new highs came on 3.16M and 3.75M shares against a ~2M norm. OBV confirms with no divergence.
  • Distance from invalidation: The daily SuperTrend stop and the 50 SMA both sit near 1186, 7% below the price. The weekly stop is at 1042. The thesis doesn't break until those levels do.
