Overall assessment¶

MKSI remains in an established downtrend, but a completed daily exhaustion setup and improving short-term momentum warrant watching for a rebound. Neither currently confirms a durable bottom.

The latest verified close of 216.10 increased 2.35% from 211.14 on October 20. Nevertheless, MKSI remains 23.21% below its October 6 close of 281.41. One positive session has therefore recovered only a small portion of the recent decline.

There is also an important distinction between the closing gain and intraday strength: MKSI opened at 216.35 and closed at 216.10. The advance was gap-led, rather than a session that closed above its opening price.

The central tension is:

  • Trend evidence remains bearish: price is below both selected moving averages, and SuperTrend is DOWN across weekly, monthly, and daily timeframes.
  • Short-term exhaustion is becoming relevant: daily TD-9 has completed a buy setup, while RSI has recovered above 30.
  • Confirmation remains insufficient: higher-timeframe exhaustion setups are still early, and the latest rebound has not repaired the broader price or volume trend.

Likewise, the current daily SuperTrend is 33.05 above the latest close. It is a bearish-trend invalidation reference, not a suitably tight stop for every short-term setup.
