  • Trend: SuperTrend is DOWN on the weekly, monthly and daily timeframes. Price sits below the 10 EMA, 50 SMA and 200 SMA, which are stacked in that order (10 EMA 219.58, 50 SMA 256.90, 200 SMA 277.22).
