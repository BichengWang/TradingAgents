Executive assessment¶

The central issue is whether MKSI can turn this recovery into sustained price acceptance above nearby overhead levels. At present:

  • Price remains below both the 50-day and 200-day moving averages.
  • Weekly, monthly, and daily SuperTrend readings are all bearish.
  • RSI has recovered from oversold territory but remains below its neutral midpoint.
  • Volume participation has improved only partially.
  • Neither TD-9 nor the multi-timeframe Z-score provides a completed or extreme exhaustion signal.

Working interpretation: an oversold-recovery attempt within a broader downtrend—not yet a confirmed bullish reversal. The observations below provide conditional decision points rather than a final trade verdict.

The eight selected indicators are:

  6. obv — tests whether participation supports the price rebound.

  8. z_score — measures statistical stretch separately from TD-9’s sequential count.

This avoids stacking several similar momentum oscillators. TD-9 and Z-score are complementary: one measures a price sequence; the other measures distance from a historical mean.

ATR measures recent range; it is not a forecast that the next session will move exactly that amount.

For evaluating a potential MKSI setup:

  • Very tight stops can sit inside ordinary daily fluctuation.
  • A wider, structurally motivated stop requires fewer shares for the same dollar-risk budget.
  • Gap risk and execution slippage can make realized losses exceed a planned stop distance.

A useful asymmetry check illustrates why entry location matters. Using 216.17 only as a reference, a hypothetical stop at 192.00, just below the recent intraday low, implies 24.17 per share of risk. The distance to the current daily SuperTrend checkpoint at 234.54 is 18.37, approximately 0.76 times that risk, before costs.

That is not an order recommendation. It shows that a rebound thesis alone does not guarantee attractive reward relative to structural risk.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = chosen dollar-risk budget ÷ absolute entry-to-stop distance
