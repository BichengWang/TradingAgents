7. MU volatility and risk management¶

The verified ATR is 49.62 per share, approximately 5.8% of the latest closing price, calculated as ATR divided by close.

That is a substantial volatility budget. A very tight percentage stop could sit well inside ordinary daily fluctuation rather than represent meaningful technical invalidation.

For scale:

  • 1 ATR: approximately 49.62 per share
  • 1.5 ATR: approximately 74.42 per share
  • 2 ATR: approximately 99.23 per share

These are volatility distances, not automatically appropriate stop placements. Invalidation should come from the strategy’s price structure and timeframe; ATR helps determine whether the resulting exposure is manageable.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted dollar risk ÷ entry-to-stop distance

Gap risk, slippage, fees, and short-sale constraints can make realized losses larger than the planned amount. Closing-signal invalidation and an executable stop order are also not equivalent.
