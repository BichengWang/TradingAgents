Actual sizing also needs liquidity, slippage, gap risk, portfolio exposure, and capital constraints. Those inputs are not available here.

For an existing MU holding: distinguish a daily trading position from a weekly trend position. The higher-timeframe stops imply substantially wider drawdowns; changing horizons after a loss would change the original risk plan.
