6. MU risk management: volatility and reward-to-risk matter¶

The verified ATR is 46.72, approximately 5.1% of MU’s latest close. ATR measures average true range, including gaps; it is not a forecast or confidence interval for the next session.

This volatility makes narrowly spaced stops vulnerable to ordinary movement. Conversely, simply using a distant higher-timeframe stop can create excessive dollar risk for a shorter-term setup.

An illustrative rebound calculation demonstrates why confirmation alone does not guarantee an attractive trade:

  • Hypothetical entry/reference: 953.19
  • Hypothetical invalidation reference: 913.60
  • Distance at risk: 39.59 per share
  • Upside to the current 50-day SMA, 982.41: 29.22 per share
  • Reward-to-risk to that first reference: approximately 0.74:1, before costs

This is not a proposed order. It shows that a reclaim of the previous close could still offer limited room before the next recovery benchmark. Adding a volatility buffer below the low would increase that risk further.

A basic sizing framework is:

Position size ≈ permitted dollar risk ÷ entry-to-invalidation distance

Allow separately for slippage, fees, and gap risk; a stop order does not guarantee the intended loss limit.
