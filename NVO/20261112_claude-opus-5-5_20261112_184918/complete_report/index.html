4. What this means for NVO¶

The first two points below are my own reasoning from the macro data, not NVO-specific evidence. The third is a limit on all of it.

  1. Valuation and rates. NVO has been valued as a long-duration growth franchise. A 10-year yield near 5% and a Fed still hiking is a headwind for such multiples. Large-cap pharma is usually lower-beta than tech and may hold up better in a risk-off move.
  2. Currency.
