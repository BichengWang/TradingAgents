6. Risk management: volatility can dominate the setup¶

Verified ATR: 22.50, equivalent to approximately 8.5% of the latest close.

ATR is a recent true-range measure—not a forecast, a maximum daily move, or a probability estimate. Nevertheless, it highlights the danger of using an arbitrarily tight stop in COHR.

Practical implications:

  • Size positions from the actual entry-to-stop distance, not from a desired share count.
  • Check whether the proposed stop is sensible relative to ATR and the observed price structure.
  • Do not assume that the 8.03 distance to the weekly trailing stop represents the maximum risk of a long position. A weekly-close rule can permit substantial movement before confirmation.
  • Allow for gaps, slippage, and stop execution away from the intended price.
  • For short positions, incorporate rebound/squeeze risk, borrow availability, and carrying costs.

A basic sizing framework is:

Position size = maximum dollar risk ÷ absolute entry-to-stop distance

If a structure-aware stop requires more room, the response is a smaller position—or no trade—not an artificially tighter stop.
