Overall assessment¶

COHR remains in a bearish broader trend, but its recent directional strength has weakened substantially. The rebound from early November has improved momentum without establishing a confirmed uptrend. At the same time, declining trend strength makes an immediate continuation of the selloff less straightforward than the bearish trend indicators alone suggest.

The evidence favors distinguishing three conditions:

  • Broader regime: Bearish, with weekly, monthly, and daily SuperTrend signals all pointing down.
  • Near-term condition: An unconfirmed rebound that has lost some momentum.
  • Trading environment: High volatility combined with weak current directional strength—a combination vulnerable to false breakouts and sharp reversals.

For COHR, neither “the bottom is confirmed” nor “the next breakdown is inevitable” is supported by the available data.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size ≈ acceptable dollar loss ÷ per-share risk, with an allowance for slippage and gaps.
