6. Risk management and limitations¶

  • Match risk to timeframe. The supplemental weekly SuperTrend stop is approximately 24.19 below the verified close. Using that as protection implies much wider per-share risk than many short-term setups.
  • Define invalidation before position size. For a long setup, a basic sizing relationship is: Shares = maximum acceptable dollar loss ÷ (entry price − protective-stop price). Round down and allow for costs, slippage, and gap risk.
  • Avoid treating a tight stop as automatically safer. A stop placed only slightly below a nearby reference may sit well inside NVDA’s current daily volatility.
  • Check bar completion. No session-completion flag was supplied. If the latest daily, weekly, or monthly bar is still forming, close-dependent signals and volume remain provisional.
  • Keep the scope clear. These tools do not assess earnings risk, news, valuation, options positioning, or portfolio suitability. The scenarios above are technical conditions for a trading decision, not an executed trade.
