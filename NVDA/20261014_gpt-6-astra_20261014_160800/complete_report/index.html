For a prospective NVDA long setup, the stronger sequence would be:

  2. A subsequent hold or successful retest.
  3. RSI recovering above 50.
  4. OBV stabilizing and then improving.

B. Bearish continuation scenario: confirm acceptance below the low¶

A bearish NVDA setup would become more credible after:

  • A daily close below 219.64;
  • A failed attempt to reclaim the breakdown area;
  • RSI remaining below 50; and
  • OBV extending lower.

Because weekly SuperTrend remains UP, a short NVDA position would still be trading against the primary higher-timeframe trend.

C. No-confirmation scenario: avoid forcing a trade¶

If NVDA oscillates around 219.64–223.09 without sustained follow-through, the low ADX reading argues for patience.

For an existing NVDA position, separate the intended holding period from the indicator hierarchy. A weekly UP regime does not eliminate daily drawdown risk, and a distant monthly trailing stop should not automatically become the risk limit for a short-term trade.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = maximum planned dollar loss ÷ per-share entry-to-stop risk

Allow for slippage and gaps; realized losses can exceed the planned stop distance.

The clearest next developments to monitor are a sustained recovery above 223.09 with improving momentum and volume, or a confirmed breakdown below 219.64.
