A basic long-position sizing framework is:

Shares = risk budget ÷ (planned entry − planned stop)

Allow separately for fees, slippage and gap risk. For perspective, two current ATRs equal approximately 14.04 per share. That is an illustrative risk distance, not a validated stop placement.
