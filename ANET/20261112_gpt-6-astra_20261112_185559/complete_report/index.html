5. Volatility and risk: price progress has come with wide fluctuations¶

Verified ATR is 11.5853, approximately 5.66% of the current ANET close.

ATR was selected because the size of recent fluctuations is central to assessing stop distance and position size.

At this reading:

  • 1.5 ATR ≈ 17.38
  • 2 ATR ≈ 23.17

These are volatility distances, not identified support levels or automatically valid stops.

A narrow stop may be vulnerable to ordinary fluctuation, while a broad stop requires correspondingly smaller exposure for a fixed risk budget:

Position size = permitted monetary risk ÷ entry-to-stop distance

Stop placement should first reflect the setup’s invalidation point and intended holding period. ATR then helps assess whether that distance is realistic. Gaps and slippage can produce losses beyond a planned stop.
