  • 1.5 ATR ≈ 1.64 per share
  • 2 ATR ≈ 2.18 per share
  • The distance from 37.30 to the current daily SuperTrend stop of 34.52 is 2.78 per share, or approximately 2.6 ATR.

A general sizing relationship is:

Position size = intended cash risk ÷ distance between entry and protective exit

Fees, slippage, and gap risk must be considered separately. A close-confirmed technical invalidation is also different from an intraday stop order: neither guarantees execution at the model’s stated line.
