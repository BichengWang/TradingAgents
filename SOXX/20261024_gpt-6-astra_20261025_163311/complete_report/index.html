6. Risk controls and limitations¶

For any SOXX scenario, define invalidation before position size:

Position size = intended dollar risk ÷ distance from entry to stop

Use a stop distance consistent with both market structure and current volatility. Stops are not guaranteed execution prices, especially around gaps, and a distant higher-timeframe indicator line may be inappropriate for a short-horizon position.

This is a technical assessment only. The tools do not establish catalyst risk, semiconductor earnings developments, valuation, spreads, or live liquidity. No backtested success rate is claimed.

Bottom line: SOXX’s trend evidence remains bearish, while daily exhaustion creates meaningful rebound risk. The deciding trader or agent should require price-and-volume confirmation before treating that exhaustion warning as a durable reversal.
