"Rates near 5% and a December hike." A 25 bp hike is already priced at 90%. Recession odds are 2% and unemployment is 4.1%, so demand isn't the problem. The VIX is back to 18.7 and the 10-year has eased from its 5.31% peak. Higher rates compress multiples, but they don't cancel 53% revenue growth.

"Cyclical peak, with memory mix rising." Cycles are real, and revenue fell about 25% in 2008–09. But this quarter's growth was broad-based and the guide is still higher. Sequential growth moderates from about +14% to about +7% on the reported guide, which is not what a peak looks like.

"Insiders sold $169M in June." That is true, and some sales were at $700+. But I can't tell whether they were plan-based or vesting-related. The CEO bought $6.9M at about $137 and is up roughly 2.6x, and large equity grants keep executives aligned. I concede that nobody bought the dip.

"Earnings quality and cash flow." Q1 and Q2 net income was flattered by non-operating items, so I anchor on operating income, which is up 38% YoY in Q3. The vendor's $1.8B TTM FCF doesn't reconcile with nine-month FCF of $3.58B. I flag it as the one number to verify, and it is probably a definition issue.

"The chart is broken." Partly true. All three supertrend tiers are down and the bounce is fading on lower highs. I'm not claiming a bottom. The chart should set sizing and entries, not the thesis.
