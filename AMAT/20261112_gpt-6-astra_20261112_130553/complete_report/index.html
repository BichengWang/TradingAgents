For scale:

  • 1 ATR: approximately 24.58 per share
  • 1.5 ATR: approximately 36.87
  • 2 ATR: approximately 49.16

This has several practical consequences:

  • A stop only a few dollars from entry could be vulnerable to routine price fluctuation.
  • A wider structural stop should generally mean fewer shares, not a larger dollar-risk allowance.
  • ATR does not cap gap risk, and a stop order does not guarantee its execution price.

Position sizing can be framed as:

Shares = risk budget ÷ planned entry-to-stop distance, rounded down and adjusted for execution costs.
