Risk geometry matters¶

For illustration—not as an order recommendation—an entry at 23.82 with a hard stop at 22.67 would imply 1.15 per share of planned risk before fees, slippage, or gaps.

  • A move to 24.42 offers only 0.60 of upside.

  • The more distant 50-day SMA cannot be assumed reachable simply because it offers a larger theoretical payoff.

Thus, an apparent rebound opportunity does not automatically offer attractive reward relative to risk. Also, a weekly-close exit rule is not equivalent to a hard stop at the weekly line; actual loss could be larger.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted monetary risk ÷ actual entry-to-stop distance

The execution agent should recalculate all dynamic lines and assess gap exposure before applying that formula.
