5. Volatility: confirmation and position sizing matter¶

The verified ATR is 9.14838, approximately $9.15 per share. The retrieved ATR series also shows volatility rising from late-October conditions.

This is substantial relative to the nearby technical thresholds:

  • The 50-day SMA is approximately $0.98 above the verified close.
  • The reported daily SuperTrend line is $3.69 above it.

Both distances are smaller than one ATR. Consequently, a brief intraday move through either level can occur within ordinary recent volatility.

Practical implications:

  1. Prefer closing confirmation and subsequent holding behavior over an isolated intraday cross.
  2. A very tight stop can be vulnerable to normal fluctuations.
  3. A wider, structurally justified stop requires a smaller position.
  4. ATR is a recent range measure—not a predicted daily move or maximum possible loss.
  5. Gaps and slippage can cause realized losses to exceed a planned stop distance.

A basic sizing framework is:

Position size = dollar risk budget ÷ entry-to-stop distance

The stop should reflect a defensible invalidation point. It should not be tightened arbitrarily just to make the potential reward-to-risk ratio appear attractive.

Why ATR: QCOM’s recent sharp reversals make volatility-adjusted risk control essential; directional indicators alone are insufficient.
