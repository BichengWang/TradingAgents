1. Bottom line¶

NFLX is in a long downtrend, and a short-term bounce is now pushing into resistance. On the weekly and monthly charts the trend is down. 2. Why these indicators¶

Price fell from about 124 (Oct 2025) to a lowest close of 67.06 (2026-10-02), then bounced for 4 days. A setup like that needs tools that answer five questions: is the main trend intact, is momentum turning, does volume back the bounce, how volatile is it, and is the move stretched or exhausted?
