MSFT (Microsoft Corporation, NMS): Technical Analysis Report¶

1. Summary¶

MSFT is in a confirmed uptrend on every timeframe I checked. It has recovered about 92% of its October 2025 to June 2026 decline and is testing the top of that range. Trend tools (the 50 and 200 SMAs, which both point up, and SuperTrend, which says "up" on weekly, monthly and daily bars) and volume (OBV at its 30-day high on Oct 7) agree. Against that, there are several short-term warnings: the weekly TD-9 sell setup has finished its 9-count, RSI and MACD have each made lower highs while price made higher highs since August, and Oct 8 was a bearish outside day that closed below Oct 7's low. Overall: the trend favours buying dips, but the short-term risk/reward for buying right now is poor, and a pause or pullback toward the 508–518 area looks somewhat more likely than a clean breakout in the next few sessions.

10. What a Trader Can Do With This¶

The final trade decision belongs to the downstream decision-maker. Here is what the indicators support:

1. Trend bias: long, but wait for a pullback rather than chasing. - From 522.61, a stop below about 496 (under the SuperTrend and 50 SMA zone) risks about 26.6 points. A move to 549.20 gains about 26.6. That is roughly 1:1, which is weak. - An entry in the 512–518 breakout/10 EMA area with the same stop risks about 16–22 points against 31–37 points to 549.20, roughly 1.5–2.3:1. That is much better.

2. Signs the pullback is healthy (buy setup). - Price holds 508–518 on below-average volume. - RSI stays above about 52–55. - MACD stays above zero, and the histogram turns positive again after any dip. - OBV holds above its September highs (about 850–860M).

3. Warning signs (cut or hedge). - A daily close below 497 would break both the daily SuperTrend and the 50 SMA. Price would then likely fall back into the old 490–513 range, with the lower band near 484 as the next target. - The completed weekly TD-9 means a failure here could turn into several weeks of sideways or lower trading. It would still not threaten the weekly trend (stop 443.87).

4. Breakout scenario. - A daily close above about 535.7 (Oct 6 high) on volume above the roughly 22M average would cancel the outside-day warning, show the upper Bollinger band is being "ridden", and point to a test of 537.65 / 549.20. - If that happens with RSI pushing above 70, it would also start to undo the RSI divergence. That would be significant.

5. Risk sizing. - ATR is about 11.6, so normal daily swings are about ±2.2%. - A 2×ATR stop (about 23 points) fits the support structure. Size positions so a 23–26 point move against you stays within your risk budget.

6. Event risk (not checked by the tools). - The data shows large-volume gap sessions on a roughly quarterly schedule: Oct 29–30 2025, Jan 29 2026 (−10.0%, 128.9M shares), Apr 30 2026 (−3.9%, 70.9M) and Jul 30 2026 (+15.5%, 110.2M).
