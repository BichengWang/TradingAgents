5. Risk controls and limitations¶

A useful sizing framework is:

Position size = predetermined cash-risk budget ÷ distance between entry and stop

Allow for slippage and gaps: a stop order does not guarantee the intended exit price. Elevated ATR argues for reducing position size when the required structural stop is wide, rather than tightening the stop arbitrarily.

This is a technical-only assessment. The tools do not establish earnings catalysts, valuation, news risks, borrow conditions, or intraday liquidity. Monthly readings can change before month-end, and no time-of-day confirmation was supplied for the latest bar.

Bottom line for GEV: The evidence favors bearish trend with incomplete stabilization, not a confirmed reversal. The most useful next evidence is whether recovery checkpoints can be reclaimed and held with improving momentum and participation—or whether the early-November base fails.
