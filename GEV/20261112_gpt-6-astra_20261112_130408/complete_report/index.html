8. Volatility and risk management¶

The latest verified ATR is 57.7037, compared with approximately 37.30 on 2026-10-12 in the indicator history. Trading conditions have become materially more volatile.

For GEV:

  • Stops only 10–20 away are substantially smaller than one ATR and may sit within ordinary price variability.
  • Wider structural stops require smaller positions—not a larger tolerated loss.
  • ATR is neither a forecast trading range nor a maximum-loss guarantee.
  • Gap risk and slippage can cause realized losses to exceed a planned stop-based amount.

A useful sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted monetary loss ÷ entry-to-invalidation distance, with an allowance for execution costs and gap risk.

Bottom line: GEV’s primary technical evidence remains bearish, but the recovery attempt, intact monthly SuperTrend, and elevated volatility argue for selective execution rather than an unconditional trade.
