5. Risk framework and limitations¶

Trend confidence should not be confused with position-size confidence. Even with bullish alignment, QQQ can move materially against a poorly timed entry.

A practical sizing framework is:

Position size = intended dollar risk ÷ planned per-share loss between entry and stop

Allow for slippage and gaps; actual losses can exceed the planned amount. Decide whether the strategy uses an intraday stop or a closing-price invalidation, because those are different risk rules.

Two data limitations remain important:

  • OBV has a confirmed missing-date anomaly, so volume confirmation is qualified.
  • The tools do not specify whether current monthly calculations exclude an incomplete October bar. Treat monthly readings as provisional until that is clarified.

The tools also do not establish upcoming catalysts, fundamental valuation, or intraday execution conditions.

Overall: QQQ’s technical structure supports a bullish continuation framework, but the latest bar quality, tentative RSI divergence, and imperfect volume data argue for confirmation and disciplined risk—not an unconditional trade instruction.
