QQQ’s verified ATR is 11.54, approximately 1.64% of the latest close. The indicator history shows ATR at approximately 8.85 on 2026-10-13, indicating that recent trading conditions have become more volatile.

Practical implications:

  • Small recovery moves can be ordinary noise rather than trend changes.
  • Tight stops can be vulnerable to routine fluctuations.
  • Position size should reflect the distance to a defensible invalidation level, rather than using an unchanged share count as volatility rises.

For context, the distance between the latest close of 704.40 and the latest session low of 701.16 is only 3.24, less than one-third of ATR. A stop placed just below that low could therefore be relatively tight compared with recent volatility.

Illustratively, 1.5–2 ATR equals approximately 17.31–23.08 per share. Those are volatility distances, not automatic stop recommendations.

A general sizing framework is:

Position size ≈ permitted dollar risk ÷ entry-to-stop distance

Allow separately for slippage and gaps. ATR is neither a forecast of the next session’s range nor a guarantee that a protective stop will fill at its trigger.
