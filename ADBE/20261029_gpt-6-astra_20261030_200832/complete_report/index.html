5. Risk and analytical limits¶

A SuperTrend close-based trend-flip rule is not the same as an intraday protective stop. Execution should account for the strategy’s bar-closing rules, gaps, and slippage.

Position sizing should follow the actual stop selected for the setup:

Shares = permitted dollar risk ÷ absolute distance between entry and protective stop

The daily and weekly stops imply very different holding periods and risk budgets; they should not be interchanged simply to avoid recognizing a failed shorter-term setup.

This analysis covers price, technical indicators, and volume. The tools do not establish earnings timing, news catalysts, valuation, or broader market conditions. Those remain inputs for the final trade decision.
