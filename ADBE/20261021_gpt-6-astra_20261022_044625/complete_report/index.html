7. Risk framework and analytical limits¶

For ADBE, the key risk-management issue is avoiding an entry whose invalidation distance is unrealistically small relative to 8.53 ATR.

  • Define the setup’s invalidation first, then size the position.
  • Do not automatically use the distant 206.08 weekly stop for a daily swing trade.
  • An illustrative distance of 1.5 ATR is approximately 12.80 per share; that is a volatility comparison, not a recommended stop.
  • Position sizing can be expressed as: Shares = permitted dollar loss ÷ absolute entry-to-stop distance.
  • Gaps and slippage can cause realized losses to exceed the planned stop distance.
