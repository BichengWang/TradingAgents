1. Indicator selection and rationale¶

Eight complementary indicators were selected for INTC’s volatile, mixed-trend environment:

  • close_50_sma — medium-term structure: Distinguishes a pullback above a recovering trend benchmark from a more consequential breakdown.
  • supertrend — trend direction and trailing risk levels: Separates weekly, monthly, and daily conditions, which currently disagree.
  • adx — trend strength: Helps assess whether directional signals have enough persistence to justify trend-following.
  • rsi — momentum: Shows whether selling pressure represents ordinary momentum deterioration or an oversold condition.
  • atr — volatility and risk sizing: Puts nearby technical levels into perspective and helps avoid excessively tight stops.
  • obv — volume participation: Tests whether buying and selling activity supports the price movement.
  • td_9 — exhaustion: Tracks whether directional sequences are approaching completion, without assuming that an incomplete setup predicts a reversal.
  • z_score — statistical stretch: Measures how unusual the price is relative to its rolling mean on three timeframes.

These indicators answer different questions: direction, strength, momentum, volatility, participation, exhaustion, and stretch. A second fast momentum oscillator would add less useful information.

8. Risk-management implications¶

The verified ATR is 6.02, approximately 5.6% of the latest close. That remains substantial daily volatility.

The reported daily SuperTrend gap of 0.98 is much smaller than one ATR. Using it as a tight hard stop could produce frequent exits even without a lasting trend change. Conversely, a close-based exit can allow a larger intraday loss.

For any INTC position:

  • Define intraday versus closing-basis invalidation before entry.
  • Separate a trend signal from an emergency maximum-loss rule.
  • Size from the monetary risk budget and realistic stop distance: Position size ≈ risk budget ÷ per-share stop risk, allowing for costs and slippage.
  • Do not widen a stop after entry merely because the original distance was too small for current volatility.
  • Treat unverified supplementary indicator levels as requiring confirmation before execution.

Decision handoff: INTC shows weakening momentum and participation, a fragile daily trend state, and no completed exhaustion or statistical oversold signal. The technical evidence supports defending existing risk and waiting for clearer confirmation; it does not establish an immediate high-conviction long or short.
