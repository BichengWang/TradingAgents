INTC (Intel Corporation): Technical Report, October 8, 2026¶

1. Bottom line¶

INTC is in a pullback inside a large uptrend, and both the trend tiers and the momentum signals disagree with each other. The longer-term structure still looks strong. The 50 SMA is above the 200 SMA, both are rising, the monthly SuperTrend points UP, and the stock has roughly tripled over the past year. But the most recent signals have turned down. The weekly SuperTrend is DOWN, and the tool says to weight the weekly tier most heavily. The MACD line is below its signal line, RSI has dropped below 50, OBV is falling faster than price, and the daily SuperTrend stop at 106.10 is only 0.93% below today's close. The next 1–3 sessions around 106.10, then 102.8–104.7 should decide whether this is a normal dip within the trend or a deeper lower high from the September peak.

6. Scenarios and possible actions¶

Scenario A: pullback holds and the trend resumes (bullish)¶

  • Conditions: price holds 102.8–104.7 on a daily closing basis, ideally with a bullish reversal candle on falling volume. RSI stays at about 40 or higher. The MACD histogram stops getting more negative. A daily TD buy-setup 9 around mid-October would add support.
  • Confirmation: a daily close back above 110.5, then above 114.3–114.9 (back above the 10 EMA, Bollinger middle and filled gap).
  • Targets: 120–123, then 126–127.4. Reclaiming 127.4 would challenge the weekly lower high.

Scenario B: pullback deepens (bearish)¶

  • Trigger: a daily close below 106.10 flips the daily SuperTrend DOWN, so it agrees with the weekly tier. A close below the 50 SMA (about 102.8), especially on above-average volume and with the weekly close below 102.94 resetting the TD count, would confirm a deeper move.
  • Targets: 99.3 (61.8%), then 94.5–97.2 (lower band and last higher low). If 94.5 breaks, the daily higher-low sequence fails and the 85–90 base comes into view.
  • The OBV divergence and today's heavy-volume gap down make this scenario at least as likely as Scenario A in the near term.

Ideas by trader type¶

  • Existing swing longs: 106.10 (daily SuperTrend) is a tight trigger, about 0.16 ATR away, and could be hit by normal noise. A more robust stop is a daily close below the 50 SMA (about 102), about 0.85 ATR of risk from here. Consider taking partial profits or hedging into any bounce toward 110.5–114.9 if price can't close back above 114.9.
  • New longs: don't buy into falling momentum (MACD below signal, RSI under 50, OBV falling, weekly SuperTrend DOWN). Two better setups:
  • (a) A support test at 102.8–104.7 or 95.5–97.2 with a reversal candle and lighter volume. Stop about 1–1.5 ATR (6–9) below.
  • (b) A momentum reclaim: a close above 114.9. This costs a worse entry price but gives confirmation.
  • Short-term shorts and hedges: only after a close below 106.10, better below 102.8. Aim for 99.3, then 95.5–97.2, with a stop above about 111 (one ATR above the trigger) or above 114.9 for a wider stop. Keep size small: the 200 SMA and monthly trend are strongly bullish, and this stock has made very large one-day upside moves (for example April 24 and September 21).
  • Position sizing: with ATR at 6.02, a 2-ATR stop is about $12 per share. Size = dollar risk ÷ 12, so risking $1,000 means roughly 83 shares. This volatility calls for smaller positions than a typical large-cap semiconductor stock would.
