  • Establishes higher lows in price and OBV.
  • Flips the daily SuperTrend upward.

The supported technical posture for INTC is bearish trend, with a credible but unconfirmed exhaustion-driven rebound possibility. For execution, the most useful distinction is between a rebound that fails below recovery checkpoints and a rebound that holds reclaimed levels with momentum and participation confirmation. Final trade selection remains with the execution agent.
