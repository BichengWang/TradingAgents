The verified snapshot supplied the 10 EMA, 50 SMA, 200 SMA, Bollinger bands, MACD, MACD signal, RSI and ATR. I added eight indicators for complementary coverage:

  • RSI: a bounded momentum read to check whether the thrust is already overbought.
  • ATR: volatility measure for stop placement and position sizing.
  • OBV: volume-based check on whether the rally has participation behind it.
  • TD-9: exhaustion count on three timeframes.
  • Z-score: stretch gauge on three timeframes.

I skipped StochRSI and KDJ because they are redundant with RSI. I skipped +DI/−DI to stay within the eight-indicator budget. That means I can't verify the directional split behind ADX (see section 8).
