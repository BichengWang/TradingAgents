7. Risk management implications¶

Verified ATR: 17.76.

For scale:

  • 1.5 ATR ≈ 26.64
  • 2 ATR ≈ 35.52

These are volatility benchmarks, not automatic stop recommendations.

A stop placed only slightly below the 50-day average could be vulnerable to ordinary price noise. Conversely, using a distant weekly or monthly trend line without reducing position size could create much more account risk than intended.

A practical framework is:

Position size = chosen dollar risk ÷ entry-to-stop distance, with additional allowance for costs and slippage.

Choose the structural invalidation point first, check its distance against ATR, and then size the position. If the necessary stop leaves insufficient reward to the next plausible price reference, the setup may not be attractive despite a directional signal.

The latest opening gap also illustrates why stop orders cannot guarantee execution at their trigger price.
