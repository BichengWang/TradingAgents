ARM (Arm Holdings plc) – Technical Report¶

1. Summary¶

ARM is in a confirmed, strengthening downtrend. It has fallen 31.6% in three weeks, from 307.49 on 2026-10-02 to 210.33. Its close of 439.46 on 2026-06-18 is down about 52% to now.

A few things point to a possible pause or relief bounce: - The daily TD Sequential shows a completed buy setup. - RSI is just above 30. - MACD downside momentum is contracting slightly. - The monthly SuperTrend is still UP.

The higher-timeframe evidence is bearish. The weekly SuperTrend is DOWN, ADX is rising through 36, and price is below the 10 EMA, the 50 SMA and the 200 SMA. The weekly TD count is only +2 of 9. A bounce would therefore be a tactical counter-trend move unless price reclaims the resistance cluster at roughly 258–262.
