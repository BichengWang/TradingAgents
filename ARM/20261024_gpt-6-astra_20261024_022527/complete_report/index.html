7. Volatility and risk implications¶

The verified ATR is 15.45, approximately 7.35% of the latest close. ATR measures recent true range; it is neither a guaranteed daily move nor a directional forecast.

For scale:

  • 1 ATR: 15.45 per share
  • 1.5 ATR: approximately 23.18 per share
  • 2 ATR: 30.90 per share

A tight stop around a nearby reference level may therefore be vulnerable to normal movement. Conversely, a wide stop requires a smaller position.

A useful sizing framework is:

Position size = chosen monetary risk budget ÷ per-share invalidation distance

Transaction costs, slippage, and gap risk must be considered separately. A stop order does not guarantee execution at the intended price.

There is also an important asymmetry at the latest close: the daily bearish SuperTrend line is 47.80 overhead, whereas the monthly reference is only 12.16 below. That does not determine a trade, but it demonstrates why a bearish trend alone does not establish an attractive fresh short entry.
