6. Volatility and risk implications¶

For STRL, this has several practical implications:

  • Stops only a few dollars from an entry may be vulnerable to ordinary volatility.
  • Wider stops require smaller positions to maintain the same dollar risk.
  • A short-term rebound can be substantial without reversing the broader downtrend.
  • Gaps and slippage can cause realized losses to exceed a planned stop distance.

The daily SuperTrend stop at 504.96 is 92.00 above the current close, roughly three current ATRs away. It is a structural trend reference, not automatically an appropriate tactical stop.

A basic sizing framework is:

Position size = permitted dollar loss ÷ absolute distance between entry and stop

Transaction costs, slippage, and gap exposure should be considered in addition to that calculation.
