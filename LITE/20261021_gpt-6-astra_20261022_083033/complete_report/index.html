Executive assessment¶

Three distinctions matter:

  • Short-term direction remains bearish: LITE is below its falling 10-day EMA, and daily SuperTrend remains down.
  • The longer-term structure has not fully broken: LITE remains above its rising 200-day SMA, with weekly and monthly SuperTrend still up.

6. Risk management: volatility is large relative to the nearby levels¶

Verified ATR is 67.37, approximately 7.9% of the latest closing price.

This has practical consequences:

  • A stop just below 826.01 or 822.51, measured from the latest close, would be less than half an ATR away. Such a stop may be vulnerable to ordinary fluctuation.
  • The distance from 850.70 to 901.24 is only 50.54, less than one ATR. A rebound toward the first major recovery hurdle does not automatically offer attractive reward relative to an ATR-sized risk allowance.
  • Weekly and monthly SuperTrend stops are too distant to substitute casually for a swing-trade invalidation level.
  • ATR is neither a maximum expected move nor protection against gaps.

A position-sizing framework is:

Maximum shares = allowed monetary loss ÷ planned entry-to-stop distance, rounded down and adjusted for costs and possible slippage.

The stop should reflect the scenario’s invalidation point; position size should then adapt to that distance.
