RSI reached 65.49—strong, but below the conventional 70 overbought threshold. ADX does not establish direction, and one reading above 25 is less persuasive than sustained strength. That combination argues against treating a high daily z-score alone as a sell signal—or chasing the gap without a risk plan.
  • Volatility makes entry price important. ATR is 3.61. A stop roughly one ATR from an actual entry entails about 3.61 USD per share of planned risk before gaps and slippage; the appropriate share count depends on the trader’s cash-risk limit.

Levels and conditional setups¶

For a breakout approach, watch whether ON can close above October 2’s 86.45 high and retain participation on subsequent sessions. That level is a fresh test, not historically validated resistance. For a pullback approach, watch the October 2 low at 82.96 and then the October 1 high at 80.46, the two boundaries of Friday’s gap. A move back through the gap would weaken the immediate breakout case; the current 50-day SMA at 76.65 and daily SuperTrend line at 73.86 are deeper, moving risk references. Conversely, a durable weekly trend change would require ON to overcome its then-current weekly SuperTrend line, now 101.98—not merely sustain the daily rebound.

Bottom line: ON’s price, volume and momentum support a tactical bullish interpretation, but the bearish weekly SuperTrend and stretched daily z-score call for confirmation and position-size discipline.
