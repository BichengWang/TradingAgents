  • A minor intraday move through the 200-day average is not, by itself, strong evidence of a decisive breakdown.

A general SNDK sizing framework is:

Allow additional room in the risk budget for slippage, fees, and possible gaps. Protective stops do not guarantee execution at the specified price.
