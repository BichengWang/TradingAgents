1. Summary¶

SNDK is in a steep, high-momentum downtrend that has just hit a capitulation-style low. A first, small relief bounce is in progress.

  • Trend: Daily, weekly and monthly SuperTrend are all DOWN. ADX is 52.98, so the trend is very strong.
  • Stretch: Momentum and stretch gauges are oversold but not extreme. RSI is 30.01 and the weekly z-score is -1.89.
  • Exhaustion: TD-9 counts are +7 on both the daily and weekly charts, so a buy-setup completion is approaching.
  • Conflict: The trend says "don't fight it", while the stretch and exhaustion readings say "bounce risk is elevated". The trend tools sit on the higher-weighted timeframes, so the structural bias stays bearish. Only a short-term counter-trend bounce is supported so far.
